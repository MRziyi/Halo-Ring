## When you need this

Halo Ring is released under [AGPLv3](LICENSE) by default. The AGPL's
copyleft is strong: any modification you ship, deploy on a network service,
or bundle into a product must also be released under AGPLv3, with full
source code, to your users.

If your business model is incompatible with that obligation — for example,
you want to ship a proprietary product that uses Halo Ring internally, or
operate a hosted service whose own source you can't disclose — you need a
**commercial license** from the copyright holder.

## What the commercial license grants

A signed commercial license replaces the AGPLv3 grant with terms negotiated
to your use case. Standard terms include:

  1. **Proprietary derivative works** — you may modify Halo Ring and ship
     the modifications without publishing source code.
  2. **Closed-source distribution** — you may include Halo Ring (modified or
     unmodified) in a product distributed under your own proprietary license.
  3. **Hosted-service use** — you may run modified Halo Ring as a network
     service without triggering AGPLv3 §13's "source for network users"
     requirement.
  4. **Sublicensing** — limited rights to sublicense Halo Ring to your own
     customers, scoped to your product.
  5. **Trademark license** — limited use of the "Halo Ring" name and
     bilingual mark in marketing of your derivative product (always with
     attribution, never implying endorsement).

## What the commercial license does NOT grant

  - **Exclusivity.** The same license terms remain available to other
    licensees. Halo Ring itself remains AGPLv3 for all non-commercial /
    individual use.
  - **Indemnification beyond standard scope.** Custom indemnification is
     negotiable but not default.
  - **Permission to claim authorship of the upstream project.** All
    derivative works must clearly disclose "Built on / derived from
    Halo Ring (https://github.com/MRziyi/Halo-Ring)".

## Pricing

Pricing depends on:

  - Whether you're shipping the code in a consumer device, a B2B product, or
    operating it as a service;
  - Annual revenue / user count attributable to the Halo Ring component;
  - Whether you want priority support, custom feature work, or just the
    license grant.

Typical structures: one-time per-product license, annual revenue share, or
a flat per-seat / per-device fee. Start a conversation and we'll tailor.

There is **no fee for personal use, hobby projects, academic research, or
open-source forks** — those are covered by AGPLv3 at zero cost.
